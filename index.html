Why concentrate

Stop paying to ship water

Ready-to-use washer fluid is mostly water. It gets bottled in plastic jugs, loaded on trucks and driven to a store, and then you haul it home. Water weighs about 8.3 pounds a gallon.

EVOLVED skips that. The bottle holds only the concentrate. You add the water where you use it.

  • One 32 oz bottle makes as much fluid as 1,760 one-gallon jugs
  • Far less plastic to buy, store and throw away
  • One small bottle on the shelf instead of pallets of jugs

FAQ

Common questions

How much water do I add?
For the 32 oz bottle, add 1 oz of concentrate to 55 gallons of water. For the 8 oz bottle, add 1/4 oz to each gallon of water. Full directions are on the How to Mix page.
Can I use it in winter?
Not yet. The current formulas are warm-weather formulas, and the mixed fluid will freeze. Switch to a winter-rated washer fluid before temperatures drop below 32°F.
Is it certified bio-based?
We've applied to the USDA BioPreferred® Program and are waiting on their decision. We'll post an update here when we hear back.

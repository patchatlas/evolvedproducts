About

Small bottles. Big impact.

Evolved Innovations makes EVOLVED, a bio-based windshield washer fluid sold as a concentrate. We're based in Sioux Falls, South Dakota.

Why we started

For decades, washer fluid has been sold the same way: mostly water, in a single-use plastic jug, shipped by the truckload. Then you carry it home, pour it in, and throw the jug away.

We thought there was a better way. Ship only the part that does the work, and let people add their own water. You get a smaller bottle, less plastic, less freight and less to store.

What we believe

  • Better for the planet should still work great on the windshield.
  • Less waste and lower cost per gallon can go together.
  • We say only what we can back up. That's why our USDA BioPreferred® application shows as "applied for" until the USDA decides.

Selling to shops or retailers?

We work with auto parts stores, service centers and fleets. Reach out and we'll talk.

How to mix EVOLVED concentrate

A little concentrate goes a long way. The 32 oz bottle has a built-in measuring chamber, so you don't need a cup.

32 oz bottle · 55-gallon drum

Mix rate: 1 oz makes 55 gallons.

Remove the cap of the small measuring reservoir.

Squeeze the bottle to fill the small reservoir with 1 oz of concentrate.

Pour the contents of the small reservoir into a 55-gallon drum.

Fill the drum with water.

8 oz bottle · by the gallon

Mix rate: 1/4 oz makes 1 gallon. One bottle makes up to 32 gallons.

Measure 1/4 oz of concentrate (about 1.5 teaspoons).

Pour it into a clean, empty one-gallon jug.

Fill the jug with water, cap it and give it a gentle shake. Then fill your washer reservoir.

Tips

  • Use clean water. Distilled or softened water helps prevent mineral spots on the glass.
  • Label any jug or drum you mix into so nobody mistakes it for drinking water.
  • Store the concentrate capped, out of reach of children, and away from freezing temperatures.

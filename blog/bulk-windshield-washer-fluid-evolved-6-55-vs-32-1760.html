Bulk Windshield Washer Fluid: Evolved 6-55 vs 32-1760

Unlocking Efficiency: Why Bulk Windshield Washer Fluids Matter

In commercial operations, clear visibility is essential for safety and productivity. Bulk washer fluid concentrates streamline maintenance, cut costs and reduce the waste that comes with buying, shipping and storing ready-to-use jugs, which are mostly water.

Choosing the right product line can change your maintenance routine. Here's why bulk concentrates make sense:

  • Operational efficiency: fewer reorders and simpler inventory.
  • Cost-effective maintenance: concentrated formulas go further, so you buy less product and less packaging.
  • Safer driving: dependable cleaning keeps windshields clear in town and on rural routes.

Evolved 6-55: One Bottle, One Drum

The Evolved 6-55 makes one 55-gallon drum of washer fluid. It's a good fit for smaller shops, farms and regional fleets that go through a drum at a time and don't want to keep a big bottle on hand.

  • Simple to use: one bottle per drum, so there's nothing to measure.
  • Two formulas: available in BugAway and RainAway.
  • Price: $20 per bottle.

Evolved 32-1760: Built for High Volume

For operations that go through a lot of washer fluid, the Evolved 32-1760 is the workhorse. One 32 oz bottle makes up to 1,760 gallons, which is thirty-two 55-gallon drums.

  • Built-in measuring chamber: squeeze the bottle to fill the 1 oz chamber, pour it into a drum and add water.
  • Mix rate: 1 oz of concentrate makes 55 gallons.
  • Less to store: one bottle on the shelf instead of thirty-two drums of ready-to-use fluid.
  • Price: $99 per bottle, which works out to less than 6 cents per finished gallon.

Which One Should You Choose?

  • Choose the 6-55 if you mix one drum at a time or want to try EVOLVED first.
  • Choose the 32-1760 if you run a fleet, a service center or a dealership and use several drums a season. It has the lowest cost per gallon.

BugAway or RainAway?

Both sizes come in two bio-based formulas:

  • BugAway: made to remove bugs, road grime and stubborn residue. Best for summer and highway driving.
  • RainAway: made for water-beading action and clearer wet-weather visibility. Best where rain is common.

A Note on Cold Weather

BugAway and RainAway are designed for above-freezing use. Before freezing temperatures, switch to a winter-rated washer fluid or follow a methanol mixing chart for the freeze protection you need.

Concluding Insights

Choosing the right bulk washer fluid is more than a maintenance decision. It boosts efficiency and cuts waste. Look at how much fluid you use and the weather you drive in, then pick the size that fits. See the Fleet & Bulk line.

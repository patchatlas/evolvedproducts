Evolved 8-32 Windshield Washer Fluid: The Smart Choice for Summer

Revolutionizing Your Summer Drive

Summer means bugs, dust, road grime and pop-up rainstorms, and you use a lot more washer fluid. Instead of hauling jug after jug home from the store, the Evolved 8-32 lets you make your own. One 8 oz bottle of concentrate makes up to 32 gallons of washer fluid.

  • Less waste: one small bottle replaces 32 one-gallon jugs.
  • Less to store: it fits on a garage shelf.
  • Lower cost: at $24 a bottle, it works out to 75 cents per gallon.

How the 8-32 Works

The bottle has a built-in measuring chamber. Squeeze it to fill the chamber to the 1/4 oz line, pour it into a clean one-gallon jug, and fill the jug with water. That's one gallon of washer fluid, ready for your reservoir.

  • Mix rate: 1/4 oz makes 1 gallon.
  • One bottle: makes up to 32 gallons.

Summer driving means bug splatter. BugAway 8-32 is made to remove bugs, road grime and stubborn residue so you can see clearly again.

  • Removes bugs and grime: made for highway miles and road trips.
  • Loosens stubborn residue: helps clear the film that builds up over a long drive.

Summer storms come up fast. RainAway 8-32 adds water-beading action for clearer visibility in wet weather.

  • Repels rain: helps water bead up on the glass.
  • Great for rainy conditions: a good everyday pick where rain is common.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much washer fluid does one bottle make?
Up to 32 gallons. Mix 1/4 oz of concentrate per gallon of water.

Can I use it in the winter?
The formulas are designed for above-freezing use. Before freezing weather, switch to a winter-rated washer fluid or follow a methanol mixing chart for the freeze protection you need.

Will it work in my vehicle?
Yes. It's for the windshield washer reservoir of any car, truck or SUV.

Tips for the best results:
Check your fluid level regularly, measure carefully, and use clean water. Distilled or softened water helps prevent spots if you have hard water.

Concluding Insights for a Cleaner, Smarter Summer

A concentrate is a simple upgrade for summer driving: less plastic, less to carry and store, and clear glass all season. Pick BugAway for bug season or RainAway for rainy weather. See the Personal Vehicle line.
